Our Projects

Recently completed electrical, mechanical, and air conditioning installations demonstrating our expertise across commercial and retail sectors

Mechanical & Electrical Installation

Wicklow LIDL

2022 Final Project Value: €700,000

CTS Group successfully completed the full mechanical and electrical installation for the Wicklow LIDL development. The project comprised the delivery of all core M&E services required to support the operation of a modern retail facility, with works coordinated throughout the construction phase to suit operational and client requirements.

The scope included complete electrical and mechanical services installations, emergency and temporary power provisions, generator support works, external car park lighting, and the coordination and relocation of services to suit warehouse and store layouts. Mechanical and electrical services were also installed to specialist equipment including balers, air conditioning units, CCTV systems, access control, disabled refuge points, and warehouse workstations. Plant-level ducting alterations were completed to accommodate the HVDS system.

Electrical Installation

Pallet Xpress – Phase 2

September 2022 Final Electrical Contract Value: €850,000

CTS Group delivered a comprehensive electrical installation for the Pallet Xpress Phase 2 development, providing a complete power, lighting, and life-safety solution across warehouse and external areas.

Works included full earthing and bonding, main and sub-distribution boards, sub-mains cabling, containment systems, general and specialist power installations, roller shutter and dock leveller supplies, internal and external lighting, car park lighting, fire alarm and AOV wiring, data systems, lightning protection, intruder alarm wiring, and photovoltaic (PV) installation. The PV system was upgraded to provide increased capacity, with associated distribution board modifications completed to comply with updated regulations.

The project was delivered in full compliance with current standards and project specifications.

Air Conditioning, Electrical & Builders Works

2022

CTS Group delivered a complete air conditioning installation for the VHI facility in Limerick, including all associated electrical and builders' works.
