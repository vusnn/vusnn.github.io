Boilerhouse Upgrade

Osprey Hotel – Boilerhouse Upgrade

November 2024 Naas, Co. Kildare

CTS Group completed a full boilerhouse upgrade at the Osprey Hotel, delivering a modernised plant facility integrating high-efficiency boilers, a CHP unit, hot water cylinders, pipework, flues and pumps together with all associated mechanical and electrical works.

Mechanical Works:

  • Two new boilers with full pipework and flues
  • Four hot water cylinders and circulating pumps
  • Pipework insulation for boilers, cylinders and CHP
  • Exhaust air and flue works
  • CHP ventilation and silencer installation
  • Custom flue pieces

Electrical & Controls:

  • MCCB and shunt trip installations
  • BMS and control wiring
  • Cat6 installation
  • Data logger reporting
  • Additional pumps and pipework modifications
  • Auto-fill unit for heat exchanger
Contract Value: €535,890.50
