Mechanical & Electrical Installation

Urlingford Fire Station

2023 Urlingford, Co. Kilkenny

CTS Group delivered complete mechanical and electrical services for the Urlingford Fire Station, providing all systems required to support the operational requirements of a modern emergency services facility. All works were installed and commissioned to the exacting standards applicable to public safety buildings.

Mechanical Services:

  • Heating plant and LPHW systems
  • Water services and drainage
  • Ventilation systems throughout the station

Electrical Services:

  • Distribution boards and containment
  • Lighting and emergency lighting
  • Fire alarm and CCTV
  • Access control and intruder alarm
  • Disabled WC alarms
  • Lightning protection
Total M&E Value: €148,598.50
Electrical Installation

Briary Gap Library & Theatre – Macroom

May 2024 Macroom, Co. Cork

CTS Group delivered the complete electrical installation for the Briary Gap Library and Theatre, a multi-storey civic and cultural facility comprising a public library, theatre auditorium, performance spaces, back-of-house areas and associated public facilities. The project required extensive coordination due to specialist theatre containment routes and ongoing design changes.

Power & Distribution:

  • ESB meter panel installation
  • Main switchboard and sub distribution boards
  • Sub-main cabling and earthing and bonding
  • Extensive cable containment including specialist theatre routes

Lighting & Data:

  • Fibre and Cat6 cabling
  • Large-scale general and emergency lighting with control systems
  • Full theatre and production lighting including orchestra pit wiring

Life-Safety & Security:

  • Addressable fire alarm system with monitoring
  • Disabled alarm and refuge systems
  • Access control, CCTV and intruder alarm
  • PA/VA public address and voice alarm system
  • AOV interface and shunt trip installation
Contract Value: €688,238.54
Fire Alarm System Replacement

Ballymun – Cash & Carry Fire Alarm

January 2026 Ballymun, Dublin

CTS Group completed the decommissioning of the existing fire alarm system and the full supply and installation of a new fire alarm system at the Ballymun Cash & Carry facility, including all cable containment.

Variation Works:

  • Eight IO units for sprinkler integration
  • Twenty-two sounders for cold rooms
  • Replacement of damaged powerpack in Vesda system
  • Lighting installation in corridor
  • Gas works to two kitchens and telesales office boiler
  • Slamshut and gas detection on main incoming gas line
Contract Value: €350,478.00
